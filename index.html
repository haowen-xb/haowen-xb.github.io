Autonomous Bluetooth Bike Lock

All code, circuits & mountsTeam of 4ME 153 design courseSpring 2026

A lock that secures your bike when you walk away. When the Bluetooth signal from your key fob drops, the base scans for the nearest rack with an ultrasonic sensor, extends a 3D-printed scissor arm, and grips the rack.

My part

  • Wrote all of the code: Bluetooth distance detection between the two XIAO nRF52840 microcontrollers, ultrasonic distance sensing, stepper motor control, and the final integrated program, where I debugged the timing and coordination among all subsystems. The one exception was a basic ultrasonic test sketch a teammate wrote to check the sensor's feasibility.
  • Handled all circuit design, assembly, and soldering.
  • Designed and 3D-printed all the mounting parts that bolt directly onto the bike, and ran the final test of the whole system on the bike.

Results

The final build could find and grip a rack, but it fell short of our targets for speed, range, and torque, and did not yet form a true lock. Along the way we traced brownouts to missing decoupling capacitors and a damaged pin to a 5 V backfeed.

Expanding Ring Frame for a Spider Robot

Independent designHawkes Lab2026

Research project in the Hawkes Lab at UCSB. I designed the robot's expanding ring frame on my own.

My part

  • Designed a planar Hoberman-style scissor ring, including linkage geometry and drivetrain sizing.
  • Built the prototype from laser-cut and 3D-printed parts. It is assembled and running.

Key design decision

One motor drives a lead screw with opposite-hand threads, so two nuts move in opposite directions and the ring opens symmetrically. The screw is self-locking, so the ring holds its size with the motor off.

Gait-Event Detection for a Rehabilitation Exoskeleton

Module ownerResearch internship

Research internship at Buffalo Robot (Chengdu) on a rehabilitation exoskeleton paired with electrical stimulation. The stimulation only helps if it fires at the right moment in each step.

My part

  • Owned the module that detects heel strike, toe-off, and swing onset from wearable sensors and triggers the stimulator.
  • Validated detections against motion-capture ground truth and measured the delay from sensor to stimulator.

Key design decision

A finite-state machine only accepts the event that should come next in the gait cycle. That keeps false triggers low.

Microspine Claw for a Jumping Robot

Claw design ownerHawkes Lab2026

Research project in the Hawkes Lab on a squirrel-inspired robot that jumps onto tree trunks. I am responsible for the claw that grabs the bark.

My part

  • Designed two claw iterations, from a flexible printed body with hooked spines to an actuated array of spring-loaded pins.
  • Current focus: reliable release after landing.

Key design decision

The pins are dragged parallel to the bark rather than pushed straight in, which drives the pin tips into the bark. A self-locking drive holds the grip with zero power.

Firmware for a Seal-Shaped Robot

FirmwareESP32-S32026

A small robot with four motors and a speaker, controlled by an ESP32-S3.

My part

  • Wrote the Arduino firmware for motor control and audio playback.
  • Added a serial command interface to test each motor and the speaker during board bring-up.

Key design decision

The motors run from two different supply voltages, so the same command gives different speeds. The firmware applies a per-motor trim to even them out.
